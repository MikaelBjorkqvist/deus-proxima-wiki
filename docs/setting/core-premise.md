# Core Premise

### LOCKED

Human civilization was first broken by a near-universal catastrophe. The centuries of isolation, collapse, and fragmentation that followed became known as **the Silence**.

Long after the Silence began, a small number of surviving powers learned of an unexplained guiding phenomenon called **the Light** or **the Beacon**. Most human civilizations never knew that the phenomenon existed.

For the few who did, the journey toward Astraeus was a civilization-scale hail-mary attempt to reach a new beginning, regardless of the cost. Most civilizations could not attempt such a journey. Most comparable efforts failed or vanished.

The successful arrivals included the ancestors of the later Imperial civilization and several corporate or factional powers. Their journeys became known collectively as **the Passage**.

The Passage ended in the **Astraeus Reach**, an isolated cluster of approximately eighteen star systems in a distant, unnamed galaxy.

By the campaign era:

- small civilizations and faction footholds have been established;
- limited local interstellar travel has returned;
- some routes and political boundaries are known;
- much of the Reach remains unexplored, inaccessible, disputed, or poorly understood;
- the wider old human sphere is functionally dead to the setting.

The setting does **not** begin with renewed contact across the wider galaxy. Reconnection is local to Astraeus.

### LOCKED — Condensed Premise

> Humanity was broken by the Great Wail and scattered through the Silence. A few desperate powers later followed the Light—or the Beacon—through the Passage and reached the isolated Astraeus Reach. Centuries later, their divided descendants are slowly reconnecting while most of the Reach remains unknown.
