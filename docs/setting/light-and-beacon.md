# The Light and the Beacon

### LOCKED — Creator Rule

The true nature of the guiding phenomenon is the **forever secret** of Deus Proxima.

It must remain permanently debatable. It may be interpreted as:

- a navigational phenomenon;
- an intelligence;
- a divine revelation;
- an artifact of physics;
- an ancient machine;
- a psychic presence;
- a projection of human need;
- a trap, rescue, summons, selection, or coincidence.

It is simultaneously “not a god” and “a god,” depending on interpretation. No final canonical explanation should collapse this ambiguity.

### LOCKED — Evidence Rule

There is no objective, universally accepted proof of what the Light or Beacon truly is.

Characters and factions may possess records, visions, artifacts, patterns, testimonies, or apparent effects that they regard as evidence. None of these establishes an uncontested answer. Institutions curate, suppress, reinterpret, and weaponize such material.

The ambiguity does not require that nothing ever happens. It means that every apparent sign remains open to multiple coherent interpretations.

### LOCKED — Revelation Rule

> **No revelation may eliminate more interpretations of the Light or Beacon than it creates.**

A discovery may reshape the debate, strengthen a faction’s doctrine, or undermine an accepted history. It must not settle the phenomenon’s ultimate identity, intention, consciousness, or divinity.

### LOCKED — Central Questions

- Why did it guide survivors to Astraeus?
- Did it guide every expedition in the same way?
- Was humanity rescued, selected, exiled, harvested, summoned, or merely redirected?
- Was Astraeus the intended destination?
- Is the phenomenon related to the Great Wail?
- Is it now silent, or are humans simply unable to understand it?
