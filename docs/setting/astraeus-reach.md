# The Astraeus Reach

# **The Astraeus Reach**
*An isolated cluster of approximately eighteen star systems.*

- **Astraeus Reach** is the locked regional name.
- The Reach is large enough to contain many cultures, governments, faiths, corporations, ruins, and frontier regions.
- It remains small enough to function as a coherent campaign setting.
- The wider host galaxy exists but does not currently require a name.
- Its isolation comes from extreme distance, scarce route knowledge, travel cost, and navigational difficulty.

---

## Scale

The **Astraeus Reach** is an isolated cluster of approximately **eighteen star systems**.

The exact known count may vary slightly within the fiction because some systems may be uncharted, inaccessible, disputed, or known only through incomplete records. Eighteen remains the accepted setting-scale number.

The Reach is located within a distant, unnamed galaxy. The wider galaxy physically exists, but it is not a connected civilization or routine campaign space.

## Isolation

Astraeus is isolated primarily because of:

- immense distance from humanity’s former sphere;
- the loss of the original Passage route;
- the extreme rarity and cost of interstellar-capable vessels;
- the logistical burden of maintaining such ships;
- the small number of crews, institutions, and factions able to operate them.

The Reach is not a sealed pocket dimension, artificial universe, or magically bounded region. Its isolation is practical, technological, historical, and geographical.

## Campaign-era Astraeus

### LOCKED — Settlement Pattern

By the campaign era, the Astraeus Reach contains an established human core surrounded by unstable and poorly known systems.

There are at least two major centers of civilization:

- **Imperial Space**, centered on the Imperium and built from descendants of the first successful arrival;
- **Consortium Space**, descended from the Great Consortium and now shared by rival megacorporate states.

Their capitals, exact borders, and detailed internal structures remain unlocked.

Several nearby star systems are also settled or partially settled, forming the oldest and most politically important region of the Reach.

### LOCKED — Degree of Development

The Astraeus Reach is divided broadly into three zones:

- roughly one-third is well established;
- roughly one-third is known but unstable, weakly controlled, or only partially explored;
- roughly one-third is poorly known, inaccessible, or disputed.

This is a worldbuilding guideline rather than a perfectly measured census.

### LOCKED — Political Reality

No faction controls the whole Reach.

Major powers hold systems, colonies, stations, trade relationships, and spheres of influence, but interstellar distance and the scarcity of carrier vessels limit centralized control.

Some systems are firmly governed. Others contain only footholds, disputed claims, isolated settlements, or nominal allegiance.

### LOCKED — Population Scale

The Imperial and corporate centers are the largest population and industrial concentrations in Astraeus.

They are true centers of civilization, capable of supporting major cities, institutions, shipbuilding, military power, and interstellar politics.

However, even these centers remain smaller and more fragile than the great civilizations of the old human sphere.

### LOCKED — Frontier Character

Much of the Reach remains unknown or only partly understood.

The frontier includes:

- undeveloped systems;
- failed or isolated colonies;
- abandoned infrastructure;
- hidden communities;
- unexplored worlds and moons;
- contested territory;
- possible ruins or anomalies whose origins are not yet locked.

The Reach has a stable core, but it is far from exhausted.

### OPEN — Reserved for Later Development

- population figures;
- whether machine or posthuman societies are major recognized powers;
- whether ancient nonhuman ruins definitely exist;
