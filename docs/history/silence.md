# The Silence

**The Silence** is the prolonged civilizational condition and historical age that followed the Great Wail.

It was defined by the loss of reliable interstellar communication, travel, trade, coordination, and shared political order. Human worlds, fleets, habitats, states, and commercial networks became isolated from one another.

The Silence produced:

- stranded worlds and wandering fleets;
- fractured governments and corporate states;
- collapsing supply networks;
- lost archives and incompatible histories;
- technological decline in some civilizations;
- authoritarian consolidation in others;
- refugee movements and failed evacuation attempts;
- religious and ideological reinterpretations;
- centuries of uncertainty about whether the rest of humanity still existed.

The Silence did not unfold identically everywhere. Some societies collapsed rapidly; others maintained limited regional order for generations.
