# Master Timeline


#### 2100 — The Terran Mandate

Humanity enters a spacefaring age. Earth’s dominant nations and corporate powers establish the Terran Mandate: a shared political order intended to coordinate expansion, regulate space development, and secure humanity’s future beyond Earth.

#### 2108 — Interstellar Exploration

The first practical spike drive is developed, allowing human vessels to cross interstellar distances and reach nearby stars.

#### 2200 — The First Wave

Humanity establishes permanent colonies on the nearest habitable worlds, beginning the first sustained expansion beyond the Solar System.

#### 2240 — Metadimensional Extroversion Syndrome

Fifth-generation spacefarers begin showing symptoms of Metadimensional Extroversion Syndrome, or MES, linked to prolonged human exposure to metadimensional travel.

#### 2260 — Psychic Training

The first MES subjects are successfully trained to control their metadimensional abilities. Their psychic powers prove essential to the construction and operation of early jumpgates.

#### 2315 — The First Jumpgate

The Psionic Authority completes the first jumpgate at the edge of the Solar System, using advanced psitech and the abilities of trained psychics.

#### 2330 — The Second Wave

The Second Wave of human colonization begins. Jumpgates allow simpler vessels to cross between connected systems, while independent spike-drive ships gradually become less common.

#### 2450 — The Golden Age

Human expansion reaches its height. The Terran Mandate remains dominant in the core worlds, while distant colonies develop into independent states, alliances, corporate powers, and rival civilizations. Nearly every form of human society and ideology can be found somewhere across colonized space.

#### 2500 — Jumpgates Reach the Frontier

Jumpgate networks spread into the colonial frontier, connecting major systems across human space. Independent spike-drive ships become increasingly rare and antiquated.

#### 2665 — The Great Wail and the Silence

The Great Wail, described only as a sound of agony that even the deaf could hear, sweeps through human space in seventeen minutes. Psychics are left dead or hopelessly insane. Machines fail, the jumpgate network collapses, and a period of stellar isolation and chaos begins.

The centuries that follow become known as the Silence. Most worlds die without their supply networks and spike-drive ships. Only some self-sufficient frontier worlds survive, along with a few enormous fleets left drifting aimlessly in the darkness.

#### 2925 — First Manifestation

After wandering aimlessly for more than three centuries, the Imperial fleet witnesses a beacon of hope emerge from the darkness. It gives the Imperials guidance and renewed purpose, becoming the foundation of what is now known as the Holy Mandate.

#### 2927 — The Emperor

The grand praetor of the Imperial fleet is elevated, immortalized, and prepared to become Emperor of the remaining Imperials: a living symbol of hope in the darkness and the supreme bearer of the Holy Mandate.

The fleet commits its remaining metadimensional knowledge and highest surviving technological resources to preserving the Emperor’s life and modifying the fleet and its spike drives for a one-way journey to follow the beacon and carry out the Holy Mandate.

#### 2933 — The Great Consortium

Through espionage, the surviving major megacorporate states learn of the Imperial Holy Mandate and its preparations for the Passage. The Great Consortium is formed from these corporations to pool ships, industry, knowledge, and personnel, securing a Passage of their own and their continued existence amid the chaos of the Silence.

#### 2951 — The Passage Begins

After twenty-four years of preparation, the Imperial fleet begins its one-way Passage, following the beacon.

#### 3007 — First Arrival

The Imperial fleet arrives in Astraeus and begins colonizing Proxima Prime and the surrounding systems, claiming the Astraeus Reach by authority of the Holy Mandate.

#### 3021 — Second Arrival

The Great Consortium arrives in Astraeus and establishes a foothold of its own. The Imperium views the newcomers as a grave threat and rejects any claim that they were also chosen by the beacon.

With the shared goal of the Passage fulfilled, the megacorporate states return to their old rivalries. Despite this division, they coexist within the emerging Consortium Space, while the Imperium and Consortium settle into a cold war and the first invisible borders are drawn.

#### 3023 — The Consortium Fractures

The Great Consortium ceases to function as a unified alliance soon after arrival. With the Passage complete, its founding megacorporate states return to rivalry while colonizing Ameena and the surrounding systems.

MantiCORE, originally created before the Passage to unify and protect the Consortium effort, is separated from direct megacorporate control by mutual agreement. It becomes an independent and necessary power, preserving interstellar access and helping prevent open war between the megacorporate states under the growing shadow of the Imperium.

#### 3021–3200 — The Astraeus Flux

Rapid colonization and expansion reshape the Astraeus Reach for nearly two centuries. Megacities, fortress worlds, automated factories, mining operations, and remote outposts spread across the settled systems.

Much of the highest technology is lost, damaged, or rendered obsolete during the Passage. Interstellar-capable vessels remain rare and become the chief limitation on further expansion.

New factions, smaller corporations, independent colonies, and frontier ventures emerge in the race for Astraeus resources. Toward the end of the Flux, the balance of power shifts less often, and the borders between regions begin to take more stable form.

### LOCKED — Timeline Notes

- The Imperial Passage lasts fifty-six years, from 2951 to 3007, and is remembered as the Great Drill.
- The Imperium has a fourteen-year advantage in Astraeus before the Consortium arrives.
- The Mandate War and its xeno-war timeline block have been removed from canon.
