# The Great Wail

**The Great Wail** is the common historical name for the near-universal catastrophe that broke interstellar human civilization.

Its formal technical designation is **the Cognitive-Mechanical Cascade**.

The Great Wail struck human minds and advanced technology at the same time. Ordinary people experienced it as an unbearable intrusion within the mind: like a cry, pressure, or psychic agony that could not be shut out. Psychics suffered the most severe neurological and psychological effects.

Complex machines and interstellar infrastructure failed as though struck by an impossible civilization-wide electromagnetic and cognitive disruption.

The event lasted only moments. Its consequences lasted for centuries.

> **First came the Great Wail. Then came the Silence.**

## Scope and uncertainty

The Great Wail was experienced or recorded across nearly all known human space. Its occurrence is not generally disputed, but surviving cultures do not agree on its cause, precise mechanism, or whether it unfolded identically everywhere.

The following remain unresolved:

- whether the event was truly simultaneous everywhere;
- whether it was natural, artificial, psychic, technological, divine, or some combination;
- whether anyone predicted or deliberately caused it;
- whether it is related to the Light or Beacon.

No current faction possesses uncontested proof.
