# The First Manifestation

After centuries of isolation and survival, the ancestors of the later Imperial civilization encountered the phenomenon now called the Light or the Beacon.

According to generally accepted history, knowledge of this manifestation was initially confined to the Imperial fleet. This is not creator-confirmed truth. Other manifestations may have occurred, been concealed, or left no surviving record.

The Imperial encounter is the earliest widely known and documented manifestation in surviving Astraeus history.

Several powerful corporate states later learned of the manifestation and the intended journey through espionage and prepared their own attempt.

> The Passage was not a shared evacuation of humanity. It was a closely guarded possibility known to only a few surviving powers—or so the surviving records suggest.

## The Passage

**The Passage** is the collective name for the extraordinary journeys that brought the first successful human factions to the Astraeus Reach.

It was a desperate, civilization-scale hail-mary escape from the conditions created by the Silence.

The Passage was not:

- an organized migration of humanity as a whole;
- a publicly available colonization project;
- an ordinary or reliably repeatable drill;
- a journey with a guaranteed destination or outcome.

The participating powers committed enormous portions of their remaining vessels, technology, personnel, knowledge, and resources.

Most human civilizations never knew about the Light or Beacon. Most did not know Astraeus existed. Most lacked the means to attempt the journey.

The original timeline establishes two major successful movements:

1. the ancestors of the later Imperial civilization followed the Light or Beacon and reached Astraeus first;
2. several corporate powers learned of the plan through espionage, organized their own effort, and arrived later.

The final names of rulers, fleets, orders, corporations, military organizations, and operations remain unlocked for later faction and timeline work.

## The old human sphere

After the Passage, the old human sphere ceases to be an active part of the setting.

Whether distant human civilizations physically survived is unknown and presently unimportant. No dependable route back exists, no meaningful communication remains, and no outside civilization is expected to resolve Astraeus’s conflicts.

> **Astraeus is the world of the setting. The old world is functionally dead.**
