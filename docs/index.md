# DEUS PROXIMA

**A science-fiction universe.**

Humanity was broken by the **Great Wail** and scattered through the **Silence**. A few desperate powers later followed the **Light**—or the **Beacon**—through the **Passage** and reached the isolated **Astraeus Reach**. Centuries later, their divided descendants are slowly reconnecting while most of the Reach remains unknown.

## Enter the Astraeus Reach

The Astraeus Reach is an isolated cluster of approximately eighteen star systems in a distant, unnamed galaxy. Its established core is divided chiefly between **Imperial Space** and **Consortium Space**, while much of the surrounding frontier remains unstable, inaccessible, or poorly understood.
