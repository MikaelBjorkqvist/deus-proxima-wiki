# Interstellar Travel

Interstellar travel within the Astraeus Reach is possible, but only through rare and extremely expensive vessels built for that purpose.

People and computers can calculate viable flight paths. Navigational expertise still matters, but calculation alone does not grant mobility.

> **Interstellar-capable vessels are among the most valuable strategic assets in the Astraeus Reach. Routes can be calculated; the means to cross them cannot be easily obtained.**

Such vessels may require:

- enormous energy reserves;
- specialized drive systems;
- rare materials and replacement components;
- highly trained crews;
- escort and support craft;
- extensive maintenance infrastructure;
- large-scale logistical preparation;
- political, military, corporate, or religious backing.

Owning or controlling an interstellar-capable vessel is a major source of power.

## Access to travel

Most people cannot simply purchase passage whenever they choose.

Interstellar travel usually requires one or more of the following:

- faction sponsorship;
- military service;
- corporate employment;
- religious or diplomatic authority;
- a valuable contract;
- exceptional wealth;
- smuggling or criminal access;
- attachment to an approved expedition;
- ownership of space aboard a carrier.

Civilian passage may exist along the most established routes, but it remains costly, infrequent, and subject to schedules controlled by carrier owners.

For most inhabitants of Astraeus:

- leaving their homeworld is unusual;
- leaving their home system is exceptional;
- many never meet anyone born in another star system;
- distant news may be delayed, incomplete, manipulated, or outdated.
