# Interstellar Carrier Vessels

The largest interstellar ships may function as **carriers for entire local fleets**.

Smaller vessels can operate normally within a star system but cannot travel independently between stars. To cross between systems, they must dock with or be carried by a much larger interstellar vessel.

This creates a layered travel structure:

- local ships handle planetary, orbital, and intra-system travel;
- carrier vessels perform the rare interstellar crossing;
- merchant groups, military formations, colonists, expedition teams, and smaller ships may travel together;
- departures and arrivals are major political and economic events.

The final in-world name for this class of ship remains unlocked until starship design and faction terminology are developed.

## Strategic value

A single functioning interstellar carrier may be more strategically important than an entire local navy.

Control of such a vessel grants the ability to:

- move military forces between systems;
- relocate populations;
- establish or reinforce colonies;
- transport high-value cargo;
- open diplomatic contact;
- rescue isolated settlements;
- deny passage and thereby isolate a system;
- determine which factions can project power beyond their home system.

A faction may possess many warships yet remain trapped within one system if it lacks an interstellar carrier.

Conversely, a faction with a modest local military but control of several carriers may exert influence across much of the Reach.

## Exceptional access

Player characters may travel more widely than ordinary people because they occupy exceptional roles connected to one of these rare vessels.

Possible campaign structures include:

- crew or officers aboard a carrier;
- members of a smaller ship transported by a carrier;
- agents granted passage by a faction;
- independent operators with access rights or partial ownership;
- survivors who inherit, capture, or steal control of an interstellar-capable vessel;
- specialists necessary to keep such a vessel operational.

For characters who own or control one, the vessel is not ordinary equipment. It is a campaign-defining asset.

> **An interstellar-capable ship is a mobile state, trade route, military instrument, and lifeline.**

Losing the vessel, its crew, its sponsor, or the infrastructure needed to maintain it should have serious consequences.
