# Jumpgates

Jumpgates were built with advanced psitech and the abilities of trained psychics. The first was completed by the Psionic Authority at the edge of the Solar System in 2315.

Their spread enabled the Second Wave of colonization and made independent spike-drive vessels increasingly uncommon. The Great Wail destroyed the jumpgate network in 2665, helping turn catastrophe into centuries of isolation.
