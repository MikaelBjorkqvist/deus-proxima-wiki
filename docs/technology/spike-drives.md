# Spike Drives

Spike drives enabled the first practical interstellar exploration in 2108. They later became increasingly rare as jumpgate networks expanded, then became essential again after the Great Wail destroyed the gate network.

The Imperial fleet modified its remaining spike drives for the one-way Passage to Astraeus. The exact engineering and capabilities of Passage-era and campaign-era drives remain open for later development.
