# The Great Consortium and Consortium Space

The **Great Consortium** was the alliance of corporate powers that followed the Imperial expedition through the Passage.

After arrival, its member megacorporations returned to rivalry once their shared survival goal was complete. They did not become a single unified corporate government. Nevertheless, they continued to coexist across many of the same planets, stations, markets, and infrastructure networks.

To the general public, this wider civilization is simply **the Consortium**, and its territory is **Consortium Space**.

The megacorporations compete through contracts, technology, influence, market control, espionage, and limited force, while remaining mutually entangled enough that outsiders experience them as parts of the same broad society.

The setting's primary geographic-political contrast is therefore:

> **Imperial Space and Consortium Space.**
