# Unnamed Biotech Megacorporation

The third major megacorporate state is currently unnamed, but its role and identity are established.

It is a clean, prestigious, highly visible medical and biotech power serving both the general population and wealthy elites.

- It operates hospitals, clinics, gene banks, prosthetics centers, and elite emergency-response services.
- It provides ordinary medicine and public health across Consortium Space.
- It sells longevity treatments, tailored offspring planning, fertility services, genetic optimization, bespoke prosthetics, and premium trauma care.
- It develops human adaptation for hostile planets, stations, and low-gravity environments.
- Its public image is compassionate, advanced, trusted, and indispensable.
- Its deeper research divisions conduct hidden experimentation and control immense stores of genetic, reproductive, medical, and behavioral data.

Its moral ambiguity centers on health, longevity, and improvement: it keeps the public alive while allowing elites to buy healthier bodies, longer lives, and planned descendants.

> **It keeps Consortium Space alive while selling the wealthy a better version of life.**

Its final name remains open.
