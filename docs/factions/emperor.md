# The Emperor

The Emperor is primarily a sacred symbol of Imperial continuity rather than a publicly visible day-to-day ruler.

- The Emperor resides within a heavily protected Imperial high sanctum.
- The highest surviving Imperial technology is used to sustain the Emperor's life.
- Public appearances are nonexistent or extraordinarily rare.
- The public receives the Emperor through icons, ritual, decrees, recordings, and official interpretation.
- Day-to-day power is exercised by institutions and leaders claiming authority in the Emperor's name.

Public doctrine holds that the Emperor is the same sovereign who survived the Passage and led humanity into Astraeus. Whether this is literally true, and the Emperor's actual condition, consciousness, and agency, remain unresolved.

> **The Emperor is the living proof on which the Imperial order rests.**
