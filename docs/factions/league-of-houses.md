# The League of Houses

The **League of Houses**, usually called **the League**, is a mobile feudal-commercial power built around a single surviving Imperial Passage carrier.

The carrier reached Astraeus barely functional. Its ancient interstellar drive cannot be reproduced, and losing it would permanently reduce the Reach's surviving interstellar capacity. During the Astraeus Flux, MantiCORE led a joint reconstruction effort using Imperial relic systems, megacorporate industry, and modern replacement infrastructure built around the original drive.

The compromise intended to keep the vessel functional and politically neutral gradually became permanent. Old fleet commands, corporate ownership claims, routes, vessels, shares, and commercial offices passed into hereditary hands and became the Houses of the League.

- The carrier is Imperial in origin, corporate in reconstruction, MantiCORE-dependent in operation, and League-controlled in practice.
- The League trades across Imperial, Consortium, and independent space, allowing commerce to escape normal geographic and political boundaries.
- Neither the Imperium nor the Consortium fully trusts the League, but both rely on it to move goods, specialists, machinery, information, and rare materials between systems.
- MantiCORE retains an indispensable technical role in preserving and certifying the carrier's ancient drive and the systems surrounding it.
- The carrier belongs formally to the League, while practical command passes between rival Houses through inheritance, contract, coalition, debt, succession, and force.

Each major League captain is a great lord in all but name. A captain's domain is not land but a vessel, a route, a web of contracts, dependent crews, trade enclaves, armed retainers, and debts owed across multiple systems. Smaller League vessels travel aboard or alongside the carrier, then disperse within each system to conduct trade and fulfill House obligations.

> **A League heir inherits a vessel, a route, and the debts of three systems.**

The House that commands the carrier stands first among the Houses, because it controls the relic that allows every other House to reach its inheritance.
